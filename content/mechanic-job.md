## Getting the job

Get the mechanic job at the City Services counter. If you do not have a vehicle, use `/taxi` to travel to the white shield marker. You can return to the counter to request the job again.

## Getting to the job area

The mechanic job is based at Los Customs in central Los Santos, north of the City Services counter. The location also has a customization shop and crafting bench.

## Job vehicles and storage

Use your third eye at the garage outside the shop to obtain a job vehicle. The job stash is the toolbox at the back of Los Customs. A separate garage stores personal vehicles.

## Repairs and payment

Bring a vehicle to the back of the shop and choose **Work On Vehicle**. Inspect it, obtain the required parts from the job shop, and use repair kits when necessary. Mechanics receive scheduled pay and can invoice players for services.

## Going off duty

Use the duty location at the shop to go off duty. You must select the job again at City Services before returning to duty.
