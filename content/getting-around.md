## Taxi service

Taxi projections appear around the city as courtesy stops. Walk into a projection and press `E` to open the taxi service menu. You can select a predefined destination or travel to your GPS waypoint.

### Using the `/taxi` service

If you cannot hail a player-operated cab, `/taxi` calls a courtesy taxi to your location. Roleplay should come first, so use the courtesy service when player-to-player taxi roleplay is not available.

### Starting a route

Open your map and set a waypoint. Once the taxi arrives and you enter it, use `/taxigo` to begin traveling. The command requires a GPS waypoint or a predefined destination.

### Stopping a route

Use `/taxistop` to leave early, cancel the route, or dismiss an incoming taxi that is stuck.
