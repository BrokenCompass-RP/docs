## Roleplay rules

Power gaming means exploiting items or systems to gain an advantage. Metagaming means using outside knowledge, including streams, to enter storylines unnaturally. Keep in-character conflict inside roleplay and communicate after intense scenes when needed.

Value your character's life and allow other players meaningful choices. Random deathmatch, unjustified drive-by shootings, and robbery without sufficient roleplay are not permitted.

## Moderation issues

Contact the moderation team when a situation cannot be resolved through calm communication. Do not use reports to continue an argument or retaliate against another player.

## Medical roleplay

Serious illness, terminal illness, mental-health stories, permanent death, and other sensitive medical roleplay require communication and respect for everyone involved. Off-limits subjects remain off limits even when presented as fictional roleplay.

## Safe word

Respect a player who uses the community safe word. Pause the scene and address the concern out of character before continuing.
