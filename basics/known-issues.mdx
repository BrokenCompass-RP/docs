## Items:

### **Food and Drink**

- Sometimes your food is invisible or its a hamburger. We'll get it all to at least be a hamburger one day but that is not today.
- Sometimes your drinks are invisible or  water bottle. We'll get it all to at least be a hamburger one day but that is not today.

### Placeable Objects

- Items that place props actually have two modes, sometimes they snap right to the ground while others let place them in elevated positions. The User Interface will tell you what mode they operate with.
- On Use Object props have a grace period life time should you get disconnected. Anyone however can possibly pick them up. They will eventually get culled by the server and \*should\* get returned to you if you can't return in the grace period window. 
- Large objects and any other large objects that spawn with an item use can sometimes cause issues. You can place them without moving and reposition as needed by picking them up again. Sometimes making them pawn further way from you is also a problem for clarity. We're picking the middle as best we can.
