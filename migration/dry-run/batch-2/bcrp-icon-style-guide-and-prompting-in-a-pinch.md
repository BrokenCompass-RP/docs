## Overall Style

### ✒️ Inked Storybook Illustration

The illustrations rely on confident black ink outlines rather than painterly edges. Every object is defined by expressive linework that varies in thickness, giving the artwork a handcrafted feel instead of looking digitally perfect.

### 🎨 Watercolor Marker Rendering

Color isn't airbrushed or hyper-realistic. Instead it feels like alcohol markers or watercolor washes layered beneath the ink.

Characteristics include:

- soft gradients

- visible brush variation

- uneven saturation

- warm, slightly muted palette

- colors that stop just inside the ink lines

The shading feels painted rather than generated.

### 🖋 Expressive Imperfection

Nothing is perfectly geometric.

Instead:

- slightly uneven perspective

- hand-drawn curves

- tiny line wobble

- imperfect symmetry

- intentionally organic construction

That imperfection is what sells the charm.

### 📚 Storybook Prop Design

The objects aren't generic clip art.

Instead they're designed like props that belong in a living world.

Examples:

- coffee cup has a custom sleeve

- orange juice carton has playful typography

- notebook has coffee stains and handwriting

- every item suggests history and use

Nothing feels sterile.

### ☀️ Warm, Optimistic Palette

The colors lean toward

- oranges

- creams

- warm yellows

- earthy browns

- muted greens

Even metallic or glass objects stay warm rather than cool.

### ✨ Stylized Realism

Objects are believable but simplified.

Not realistic enough to be photographs.

Not cartoony enough to be comic icons.

Instead they resemble illustrations you'd see in:

- tabletop RPG rulebooks

- indie cozy games

- illustrated cookbooks

- Zelda concept art

- hand-painted board game components

### 💡 High Readability

Probably the strongest characteristic.

Every icon is immediately readable at inventory size.

To achieve that:

- bold silhouette

- large recognizable shapes

- limited visual clutter

- exaggerated proportions where helpful

- thick outlines separating adjacent objects

This is excellent for FiveM inventory usage.

### 🪟 Transparent Background

Every icon floats naturally.

No circle.

No square.

No shadow plate.

No decorative border.

Just the object with a subtle contact shadow if needed.

# Prompt Template

You are a world-renowned fantasy game illustrator creating premium inventory icons for a modern FiveM roleplay server. Create a highly readable square-format inventory icon with a transparent background.

Illustrate [SUBJECT] using expressive hand-drawn black ink linework with varied line weight, subtle sketch imperfections, and organic, slightly irregular shapes that feel traditionally illustrated rather than digitally perfect. Apply soft watercolor and alcohol-marker style rendering using warm, slightly muted colors with gentle painterly gradients and visible brush variation. Use bold silhouettes, simplified forms, and exaggerated readability so the icon remains instantly recognizable even when displayed at small inventory sizes.

The object should feel like a real, well-used prop from a living world, showing subtle personality and believable wear without becoming dirty or overly distressed. Avoid photorealism, glossy 3D rendering, plastic textures, cel shading, anime styling, flat vector art, AI-generated smoothness, excessive detail, or harsh lighting.

Use clean black outlines, warm highlights, gentle ambient shading, and a cozy storybook aesthetic inspired by hand-illustrated RPG manuals and tabletop game components. Maintain generous negative space around the subject. Center the object within the canvas and export as a 1:1 square icon with a fully transparent background, optimized for FiveM inventory use.
